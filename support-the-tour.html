Questions about giving?

Find details about our mission, tax status, online gifts, ACH transfers, and checks.

Our mission is to spark an intersectional, youth-led movement that fights for our right to vote and a just democracy for all.

Generation Vote Education Fund is a fiscally sponsored 501(c)(3) project of the Social Good Fund. It supports public education, youth voting-rights work, nonpartisan election protection, training, leadership development, and partnerships.

Generation Vote Action is a 501(c)(4) advocacy organization supporting campaigns, communications, leadership development, lobbying, and candidate endorsements within applicable legal limits.

Gifts to the Education Fund are tax-deductible; gifts to Generation Vote Action are not.

  • Generation Vote Education Fund — tax-deductible 501(c)(3) giving
  • Generation Vote Action — non-tax-deductible 501(c)(4) giving

Please email Brendan Cushing, Managing Director, at bcushing@genvote.org for instructions. GenVote coordinates incoming Education Fund transfers with its fiscal sponsor, the Social Good Fund, so please include your intended gift and timing.

Generation Vote Education Fund

  • Make the check payable to “Social Good Fund.”
  • Write “For the Generation Vote Education Fund” in the memo line.
  • Mail to: Brendan Cushing, Generation Vote Education Fund, 367 S 4th Street, Apt 3, Brooklyn, NY 11211.

Generation Vote Action

  • Make the check payable to “Generation Vote Action.”
  • Mail to: 4007 73rd St, Apt 4B, Woodside, NY 11377.

Grassroots donations support young GenVoters through organizer training, leadership development, local teams and hubs, electoral justice campaigns, direct actions, and community engagement. Gifts help cover materials, technology, food, travel, and training expenses.
